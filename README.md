# Online Store UI in Xamarin Forms


This Xamarin.Forms sample demonstrates how to build a modern **Online Store Product Details UI** using Xamarin.Forms, Syncfusion controls, and PancakeView. The sample showcases a visually appealing product details page that enables users to explore product information, manage quantities, browse related products, and add items to their shopping cart through an intuitive mobile shopping experience.

The application is designed to present product information in a clean and engaging manner. A large product image is displayed at the top of the page, providing immediate visual focus for the selected item. Navigation and action buttons are positioned over the image, allowing users to navigate back or access additional options without disrupting the shopping experience.

The product details section displays important information such as the product title, quantity selection, price, description, related products, and key features. The layout uses a modern card-based design with rounded corners to create a polished appearance commonly found in contemporary e-commerce applications.

The sample utilizes Syncfusion's **SfNumericUpDown** control to manage product quantities. Users can easily increase or decrease the number of items they wish to purchase using dedicated increment and decrement buttons. This touch-friendly interaction improves usability and provides a seamless shopping experience on mobile devices.

A collection of related products is displayed using a horizontal CollectionView. This section encourages product discovery by showcasing additional items that may interest customers. Product images are presented in a visually attractive format, helping users browse related merchandise without leaving the current page.

The bottom section of the interface contains a floating action button that provides quick access to shopping actions. Combined with detailed product information and quantity management, the page delivers a complete product exploration and purchasing experience.
